## Q1: [Criminal Defense] What documents do foreigners need to work in China?

**A**: Foreigners working in China need a work permit and work-type residence permit. Process: 1) Employer applies for Foreigner Work Permit Notice; 2) Foreigner applies for Z visa at Chinese embassy; 3) Within 30 days of entry, obtain Work Permit; 4) Apply for residence permit.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国刑法》

**领域**: 刑事辩护 | **语言**: English

## Q2: For Overseas Chinese: How is property divided in cross-border divorce?

**A**: Cross-border divorce property division involves multiple legal systems: 1) Determine jurisdiction court first; 2) Chinese property follows Chinese law, overseas property may follow local law; 3) Different countries have different standards for marital property; 4) Prenuptial agreements recommended.

**法律依据**: 《中华人民共和国民法典》婚姻家庭编

**领域**: 婚姻家事 | **语言**: English
